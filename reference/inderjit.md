# Inderjit dose-response data

Example data from Inderjit et al. (2002) on the dose-response of
herbicide on perennial ryegrass growth.

## Usage

``` r
inderjit
```

## Format

A data frame with 24 rows and 2 columns:

- response:

  Ryegrass root length

- dose:

  Herbicide dose

## References

Inderjit, Streibig JC, & Olofsdotter M. (2002). Joint action of phenolic
acid mixtures and its significance in allelopathy research. *Physiologia
Plantarum*, 114(3), 422-428.
